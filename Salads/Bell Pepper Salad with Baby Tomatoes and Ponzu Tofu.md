# Ingredients

- 8 garlic cloves cut through, to rub the bowl
- 4 each red, yellow and green capsicum
- 48 baby Roma tomatoes, halved
- 8 slim spring onions, finely chopped
- ~600g (2 blocks) medium-firm or firm tofu, patted dry and cut into ~1.5cm cubes (in place of feta; see Notes)
- Handful of fresh basil leaves, torn
- Toasted sesame seeds and/or shredded nori, for the tofu (optional)
- Flaky salt, to finish

## Dressing

- 12 cloves of garlic, finely chopped
- 4 tablespoons seasoned rice vinegar
- 4 teaspoons Tamari soy sauce
- 4 tablespoons ponzu
- 8 tablespoons finely chopped sushi ginger
- Black pepper to taste

## Extra for marinating the tofu

- 4 tablespoons ponzu
- 2 teaspoons Tamari soy sauce
- 2 teaspoons grated fresh ginger

# Method

1. **At least 30 minutes ahead** (up to a few hours, in the fridge): Mix the dressing ingredients in a bowl. In a separate shallow dish, combine a third of the dressing with the extra marinade ingredients, add the tofu cubes and turn gently to coat. Leave to marinate, turning occasionally.
2. Slice right through two connected garlic cloves (i.e. stuck together) and rub the inside of the bowl all over vigorously.
3. Separate the capsicums in their natural sections (follow the spines as it were) and remove all the unwanted bits and pieces. Slice into thin strips. Place them around the bowl, in unison.
4. Top them with halved baby Roma tomatoes and sprinkle finely chopped spring onion over.
5. Gently toss the marinated tofu with the torn basil and spoon it, with a little of its marinade, into the centre of the salad. Finish the tofu with a pinch of flaky salt and the sesame seeds and/or nori, if using.
6. Drizzle the remaining dressing all over the salad. Drizzle some extra ponzu sauce around the exposed parts of the peppers.

# Notes

- Made by Linda at a Christmas lunch. The ponzu sauce is the real star of the show here, making for a rather surprising flavour combination.
- The original used feta. The tofu replacing it is inspired by Japanese _hiyayakko_ (chilled tofu dressed with soy/ponzu, ginger and spring onion), which shares almost all its seasonings with this dressing. Marinating seasons the tofu throughout; for a softer, more traditional take, use chilled silken tofu and spoon the marinade over just before serving.
