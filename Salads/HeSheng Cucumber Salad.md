# Ingredients

- 1 large cucumber
- 1 small clove garlic, crushed & chopped
- small handful coriander (de-stemmed, roughly chopped)
- salt, MSG
- tiny splash rice vinegar

# Method

1. Peel and quarter (length-ways) the cucumber, then remove seeds.
2. Slice each strip in half and smash each piece with a knife. Cut the strips into ~2cm pieces.
3. Prepare a brine by dissolving a generous amount of salt and MSG (to taste) in 3 cups of hot water. Pour hot brine over the cucumbers, and allow to sit for ~30min.
4. Drain brine (not too thoroughly), and transfer cucumbers to a narrow bowl. Top with garlic in a heap.
5. Heat the smallest amount of oil possible to smoke point, and pour searing oil over garlic — as little as possible while ensuring adequate searing.
6. Heat a small amount of water (just enough to serve as the salad broth, so less than even covers the bottom of the small saucepan) and dissolve some salt (small pinch; not too much), MSG (generous amount), and sugar (very little; tip of a spoon). Pour over salad.
7. Add coriander, and 1/2-1tsp rice vinegar (add slowly while tasting).
8. Refrigerate until cold.
