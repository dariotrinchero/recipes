# Ingredients

- 1 1/2 Cup Water (room temp)
- 2 Cups unsweetened soy milk, reduced to 1 cup (in place of evaporated milk; see Method)
- 2 Tbsp Sugar (use less if you want)
- 3 tsp Loose Strong Black Tea
- 3-4 g Fresh Ginger
- 4 Green Cardamom pods
- 2 Cloves
- 8 Threads Saffron (optional)
- Piece of cinnamon

# Method

1. **Reduce the soy milk** (can be done ahead): Simmer the soy milk with the sugar in a wide pan over medium-low heat, stirring often and scraping the bottom, until reduced by half to 1 cup (20-30 minutes). A skin will form; stir it back in or skim it off. Keeps in the fridge for a few days.
2. Add the black tea to your pot with the spices. Slice the ginger into strips and add as well.
3. Pour over the room temp water and bring to a boil.
4. Reduce heat to medium and allow to simmer for 5 minutes.
5. When the time is up, add the reduced soy milk. DO NOT leave the pot as it will boil over if you do.
6. Continue to cook over medium-high heat (2-3 minutes) until the Chai comes to a boil.
7. Repeat this 3-5 times to make the texture velvety:
    1. Bring the pot to a boil so the chai foams up
    2. Remove the pot from the stove so that the foam dies down
    3. Once the pot has stopped boiling completely, place this back on the stove
8. After this, strain the tea and serve it hot or cold.

# Notes

- Reducing the soy milk with the sugar mimics the body and slight caramelised, cooked-milk flavour of evaporated milk.
- **Shortcut**: Skip the reduction and use ~1¼ cups barista-style oat milk as-is, adding the sugar in step 4 instead. Don't reduce oat milk; its starch makes it turn gloopy.
- Sweetened condensed oat or coconut milk also works in place of the reduced milk; omit the sugar if using it.
