# Ingredients

## Satay sauce

- 1/4 cup smooth peanut butter
- 2/3 tbsp cider vinegar
- 2/3 tbsp Thai red curry paste
- 1/4 cup coconut cream
- 1 tbsp sugar (plus more to taste)
- 1 tsp dark soy sauce

## Tacos

- 8 [[Soft Taco Shells]]
- 1 head cauliflower, cut into florets
- handful fresh coriander
- 4 blocks medium to firm tofu (smoked would be good), cut into 2cm×2cm squares
- 1/2 red onion, sliced
- 2 finger limes (substitute for a squeeze of lime if needed), pearls squeezed out
- sesame seeds for garnish

# Method

1. Turn on the oven to 200°C. When ready, insert cauliflower (tossed in oil and salt), and roast until brown and crispy.
2. Mix the satay sauce. Adjust to taste.
3. Marinade the tofu in a makeshift marinade (soy sauce, oil, garlic, mirin or syrup). Fry until brown.
4. Assemble tacos, piling ingredients on a layer of satay sauce. Be generous with the finger lime pearls.
