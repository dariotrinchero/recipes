# Ingredients

- 2 large leeks, finely chopped into half-moons
- olive oil, for frying
- sea salt
- 4 cloves garlic, minced
- 1 tsp ground cumin
- 1 tsp ground coriander
- 3 x 400g cans butter beans, with their liquid if organic; otherwise drained, plus 250ml vegetable stock (or water with a pinch of salt)

## Green Sauce

- 2 large handfuls kale, stems removed
- 50g cashews, soaked in boiling water for around 10 minutes
- 100g silken tofu
- 2 tbsp nutritional yeast
- 2 tbsp white miso paste
- 1 tsp olive oil
- 2 cloves garlic, minced
- juice of 1 lemon

## Tahini Sauce

- 2 tbsp runny tahini
- 1.5 tbsp fresh lemon juice
- 1-3 tbsp water

## To Serve

- 3 tbsp pumpkin seeds
- sesame seeds (optional)
- chilli flakes
- freshly cracked black pepper
- drizzle of olive oil
- toasted sourdough

# Method

1. Soak the cashews in boiling water for around 10 minutes.
2. Blanch the kale for 2 minutes in boiling water, then 1 minute in ice water. Add to a bullet blender with the soaked cashews, silken tofu, nutritional yeast, 1 tsp olive oil, miso, 2 cloves of garlic and the lemon juice. Blend until smooth, adding a splash of water if the sauce needs loosening to blend properly.
3. In a large pan over medium-low heat, caramelise the leeks in plenty of olive oil with a big pinch of salt for around 10 minutes, until they're greatly reduced in size, browning and tasting sweeter.
4. Add the remaining garlic, cumin and coriander and sauté for 3-4 minutes, mixing well. Add the butter beans with their liquid (or drained, with the stock) and stir through. Turn the heat up for a couple of minutes until the beans are bubbling, then reduce to a simmer for a further 5 minutes, until warmed through.
5. Meanwhile, whisk the tahini with the lemon juice and 1 tbsp water until smooth, adding more water until it's runny enough to drizzle. If you like, toast the pumpkin seeds in a dry frying pan with some sea salt for 2-3 minutes to make them crispy.
6. Stir the green sauce through the beans and let it bubble for 2 minutes, then take off the heat.
7. Drizzle with the tahini sauce and top with the pumpkin seeds, black pepper, chilli flakes, sesame seeds (if using) and a drizzle of olive oil. Serve with toasted sourdough.

# Notes

- Adding the miso right at the end and only heating it gently keeps its flavour and fermented properties, and balances any bitterness from the greens.
- The source claims 12mg iron, 32g protein and 21g fibre per serving.
