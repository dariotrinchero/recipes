# Ingredients

- 1 sheet nori, cut into 2cm-wide rectangles, long enough to wrap around the bottom of each onigiri

## For rice

- 1.5 cups sushi rice
- handful of wakame, blitzed to a fine powder
- splash of sesame oil
- salt, MSG, sugar
- frying oil

## For glaze

- 50g white miso
- 25g sake
- 25g sugar
- 25g mirin

# Method

1. Wash the rice and cook. Once cooked, transfer into a large bowl and stir in ~2tbsp sugar, additional salt to taste, the wakame, and a splash of sesame oil. Allow to cool completely.
2. Meanwhile, prepare the glaze by adding all ingredients to a saucepan and heating until sticky and caramel-coloured.
3. Shape the onigiri (classic rounded triangular prism shapes), about 10 in total.
4. Shallow fry the onigiri in oil, flipping carefully, to develop a nice dark crust on both sides.
5. Apply glaze to one side of the onigiri.
6. One at a time, add the onigiri into a pan of hot oil glaze side down, and immediately apply glaze to the other side. Do not fry the glaze for more than ~20s if the oil is hot; it burns fast. Flip and fry both sides.
7. Wrap a small rectangle of nori around the bottom of each onigiri to make for easier handling.
8. Serve with soy sauce for dipping.
