# Ingredients

- 75g (1/2 cup) cashews
- 2 cups reserved cashew cooking water
- 160ml (1 can) unsweetened coconut cream, whisked smooth
- juice of 1 lemon
- 2tsp tahini
- 1Tbsp vegan butter
- 2Tbsp nutritional yeast
- 1.5tsp salt
- 1/4tsp garlic powder
- 1/4tsp onion powder
- pinch fennel seeds, ground
- pinch mustard seeds, ground
- 2Tbsp pectin
- 2Tbsp tapioca flour, whisked into 1/2 cup water

# Method

1. Fill a medium saucepan halfway with water and bring to the boil over medium-high heat. Add a pinch of salt and the cashews, and boil for 20min. At the 15min mark, reserve 2 cups of the cooking water.
2. Add the boiled cashews and the reserved cooking water to a blender, along with all remaining ingredients except the pectin and the tapioca slurry. Blitz until completely liquefied.
3. Pour into a saucepan over medium-high heat. Once bubbling (about 7-8min), whisk in the pectin and cook, whisking, for a further 2min.
4. Pour in the tapioca slurry and continue to whisk for 3-4min, until the sauce thickens to the consistency of stringy melted cheese.
5. Pour into an oiled tall cylindrical glass, and set in the fridge overnight, for at least 24h.
6. Tilt and roll the glass to release the cheese from the sides, then slide it out.

# Notes

- Keeps in the fridge on a plate covered in cling-film.
- Stopping at the end of step 4 gives a pourable cheese sauce, which is worth a try over nachos or pasta.
- Intended as a vegan substitute in recipes like [[Fennel & Chilli-Spiced Mushroom, Basil, & Ricotta Pizza]] or [[Manakish]].
