# Ingredients

- 4 tablespoons extra-virgin olive oil, divided
- 1 medium-to-large yellow onion, finely chopped
- 2 carrots, peeled & finely chopped
- 2 ribs celery, finely chopped
- 4 cloves garlic, crushed
- 1 can (15 ounces) crushed tomatoes
- 4 cups vegetable broth
- 2 bay leaves
- 1 teaspoon dried oregano
- ¼ teaspoon red pepper flakes
- 2 cans (15 ounces each) cannellini beans, rinsed and drained (or 3 cups cooked beans)
- 1 cup cavatelli, ditalini, elbow or small shell pasta of choice
- 2 cups chopped Tuscan kale (tough ribs removed first), or chard or collard greens
- ¼ cup finely chopped Italian parsley
- 1 tablespoon fresh lemon juice (about ½ medium lemon)

# Method

1. In a large Dutch oven or soup pot over medium heat, warm 3 tablespoons of the olive oil until shimmering. Add the chopped onion, carrot, celery, ½ tsp of salt, and ~10 twists of black pepper. Cook, stirring often, until the vegetables have softened and the onions are turning translucent, about 6 to 10 minutes.
2. Add the garlic and cook until fragrant. Add the tomatoes, and cook until bubbling all over. Add the broth plus 3 cups of water, bay leaves, oregano, and red pepper flakes.
3. Raise the heat to medium-high and bring the mixture to a simmer. Cook for 10 minutes, stirring occasionally, and reducing the heat as necessary to maintain a gentle simmer.
4. Transfer ~1½ cups of the soup (avoiding the bay leaves) to a blender. Add ~¾ cup of drained beans. Blend until smooth, being careful to avoid hot steam escaping from the lid. Pour the blended mixture back into the soup.
5. Add remaining beans, pasta, kale & parsley. Continue cooking, stirring often, for ~20min, or until the pasta and greens are pleasantly tender.
6. Remove pot from heat, and discard bay leaves. Stir in the lemon juice and remaining tablespoon of olive oil. Season to taste and garnish with extra olive oil.

# Notes

- Leftovers taste even better the next day. Refrigerate for up to 5 days or freeze in individual portions.
