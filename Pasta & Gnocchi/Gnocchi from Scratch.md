# Ingredients

- 3 large floury potatoes (~1kg); choose ones sold for baking or mashing, not waxy ones
- bread flour or "00" flour, about 30% of the grated potato's weight (~250-300g for 1kg potato)
- vital wheat gluten, 1 tsp per 500g of potato (~2 tsp)
- generous salt & pepper
- oil or vegan butter, for pan-frying

# Method

1. Poke potatoes with fork, then microwave until soft.
2. With oven gloves, grate potatoes (skin on) on large side of box grater, directly onto the counter, while still hot. Discard skin shreds. Spread out thinly, & leave **just until they stop visibly steaming** (5-10 minutes). Most of the water evaporates in this window, while the potato is still warm (and so still sticky enough to bind well). If the potatoes seem wet, dry the grated potato further on a tray in a hot oven for a few minutes.
3. Weigh the potato and measure out flour at about 30% of its weight. Whisk the vital wheat gluten into the flour.
4. While the potato is still warm, season generously, sprinkle over about two-thirds of the flour mixture, and bring together. Add the remaining flour gradually, just until the dough is no longer sticky.
5. Knead for 1-2 minutes, until smooth and slightly elastic. (Without egg, the bite comes from gluten, so knead a little more than you would for egg-based gnocchi.)
6. **Test**: Boil a single piece. If it's soft and mushy, knead in a little more flour (or a pinch more gluten) and test again. If it's tough or rubbery, note it for next time (less gluten/kneading).
7. Roll into 4 logs, and cut into equal pieces. Optionally impression each with grooves using tines of a fork.
8. Cook gnocchi in well-salted water at a lively simmer (not a rolling boil). Remove each piece 10-20 seconds after it floats; don't let them linger.
9. Recommended: Pan-fry the drained gnocchi in oil or vegan butter over high heat until golden on two sides, for a crisp exterior.
10. Finish with sauce of your choice.

# Notes

- **Egg-free version (untested).** The original used a small egg, which gave the gnocchi firmness; previous egg-free attempts held together but had no bite (like little clumps of mash). The changes aimed at fixing that are: more & stronger flour, a little vital wheat gluten, more kneading, mixing while warm, gentler cooking, and pan-frying.
- Variations to try:
    - Replace about a quarter of the flour with fine semolina, for a firmer, slightly grainy bite.
    - Adjust the vital wheat gluten (up to ~2 tsp per 500g potato for more chew); too much makes them rubbery.
    - If the dough struggles to bind (rather than lacking bite), add 1-2 Tbsp potato starch or cornflour per 500g potato.
