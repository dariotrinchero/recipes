# Ingredients

## Pesto (makes excess)

Use shop-bought vegan pesto (Princess Pesto's vegan pesto works well), or make your own:

- 80g basil
- 40g pine nut kernels, toasted
- 2 large garlic cloves, crushed
- 2-3 tbsp nutritional yeast (in place of pecorino), to taste
- 8/9 (=(2+1/3)(2/3)) cup extra virgin olive oil
- salt & pepper to taste (quite a bit of salt is needed)

## Gnocchi sauce

- ~125g portabellini mushrooms, sliced
- thyme, dried or fresh
- 5 large(ish) brown mushrooms, sliced
- ~130g rosa tomatoes, halved
- 2 large garlic cloves, minced
- ~50g vegan butter
- ½ of a 50g sachet of tomato paste
- ~30ml coconut cream or plant-based cooking cream
- salt to taste

## Final dish

- 500g (prior to cooking) gnocchi; consider making [[Gnocchi from Scratch]] to reduce cost
- 1 small raw carrot, julienned then sliced into thin strips, for garnish
- 1/8 small raw red onion, thinly sliced, for garnish
- vegan parmesan or nutritional yeast for garnish (optional)
- fresh parsley for garnish

# Method

1. If making your own, prepare pesto by blending all ingredients well, adding oil slowly.
2. Sauté mushrooms in vegan butter with thyme and garlic. Add tomatoes and tomato paste, cooking until tomatoes begin to disintegrate. Add cream. Check seasoning.
3. Cook gnocchi until floating, fishing directly into sauce. Plate, adding garnish and two small dollops of pesto per serving.

# Notes

- The mushroom technique is very similar to [[Vegan Creamy Mushrooms (for Pasta, Gnocchi, or Risotto)]]; see its notes for tips on maximising flavour (meticulous browning, flavour boosters, etc.).
