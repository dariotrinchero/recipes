# Ingredients

## Tofu lardons

- 1 block firm tofu, cut into lardons
- 1Tbsp maple syrup
- 2Tbsp soy sauce
- 1Tbsp Maggi sauce
- 1Tbsp tomato purée
- 1tsp smoked paprika
- 1tsp garlic granules
- 1tsp onion granules
- salt & pepper

## Silken sauce

- 1 block (~300g) silken tofu
- 100g cashews
- 150ml oat milk
- 3 cloves garlic
- 3Tbsp nutritional yeast
- 1tsp miso paste
- 1/2tsp Dijon mustard
- 1tsp kala namak (Indian black salt), for an eggy flavour
- 1tsp sugar
- pinch of turmeric, for colour
- salt & plenty of coarsely cracked black pepper

## To assemble

- spaghetti, for 4
- olive oil
- 2 ladles of starchy pasta water
- handful of fresh parsley, chopped

# Method

1. Toss the tofu lardons with the syrup, soy sauce, Maggi, tomato purée, spices, salt & pepper. Air-fry until crispy.
2. Blend all of the sauce ingredients until completely smooth. Taste for salt & pepper.
3. Cook the spaghetti, reserving a mug of the starchy cooking water before draining.
4. Gently warm the sauce in a pan with a glug of olive oil, loosening it with 2 ladles of the pasta water. Remove from the heat before it over-thickens, or the spaghetti will turn stodgy.
5. Fold the cooked spaghetti and most of the parsley through the sauce.
6. Serve topped with plenty of the crispy lardons, the remaining parsley, and extra black pepper.

# Notes

- Unless you have a powerful blender, soak the cashews in boiling water for ~15min (or boil them for 10min) before blending, or the sauce will stay grainy.
- Without an air fryer, roast the lardons at 200°C for 20-25min, turning once, or pan-fry them as in [[Crispy Tofu]].
- The kala namak is what makes this read as carbonara rather than as a plain cashew cream; see also [[Imitation Egg Seasoning]].
