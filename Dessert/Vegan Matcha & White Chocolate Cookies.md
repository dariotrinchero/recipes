# Ingredients

- 250g (2 cups) plain flour
- 3Tbsp matcha powder, sifted
- 2tsp baking powder
- 1tsp bicarbonate of soda
- 65g (1/3 cup) granulated sugar
- 1/4tsp salt
- 160ml (2/3 cup) maple syrup
- 120ml (1/2 cup) neutral oil
- 2tsp vanilla extract
- 120g (2/3 cup) vegan white chocolate chips

# Method

1. Preheat the oven to 175°C, and line 2 baking trays with parchment.
2. Mix together the flour, matcha, baking powder, bicarbonate of soda, sugar, and salt.
3. Make a well in the centre of the dry ingredients, add the maple syrup, oil, and vanilla, and mix until fully incorporated.
4. Fold in the white chocolate chips.
5. Scoop the dough onto the prepared trays, leaving room to spread, and bake for 11min.
6. Cool completely on the trays — the cookies are very soft straight from the oven, and firm up as they cool.

# Notes

- Sift the matcha: 3Tbsp is a lot, and it clumps readily. Use a decent culinary grade, since a dull or stale matcha will taste hay-like and bitter at this quantity.
- An oil-based dough, so there is no creaming and no chilling — it comes together in minutes.
