# Non-vegan Purge

## Easily Veganisable

### Cheese is a topping or minor part

- [ ] [[Stuffed Peppers]]: vegan shreds, or omit.
- [ ] [[Black Bean Enchiladas]]: vegan shreds, or omit.

### Eggs only as binder, or few and replaceable

- [ ] [[Mini Herb, Olive & Buttermilk Cornbreads]]: existing #todo plan; vegan cheese or nutritional yeast for gruyère.
- [ ] [[Earl Grey, Cardamom & Orange Loaf]]: vegan butter, flax eggs.
- [ ] [[B’s Legendary Choc-Chip Cookies]]: vegan block butter, flax egg (or just use the vegan cookie recipes).
- [ ] [[Malva Pudding]]: 1 egg, milk, butter; coconut or oat cream sauce.
- [ ] [[Cardamom Buns]]: enriched dough with 1 egg; plant milk, vegan butter.
- [ ] [[Tarte Tatin]]: vegan block butter; vegan puff pastry (check label).
- [ ] [[Peach, Rosemary & Lime Galette]]: vegan crème fraîche or thick coconut yogurt; vegan puff pastry; plant milk & maple wash.

## Grey Areas

### Cheese is the star

- [ ] [[Halloumi & Aubergine Shakshouka]]: vegan grilling cheese, or brine-marinated pan-fried tofu; maple for honey.

### Egg-heavy bakes and desserts

- [ ] [[Orange & Almond Syrup Cake]]: 5 eggs give the structure; aquafaba would need testing.
- [ ] [[Fig, Yogurt, & Almond Cake]]: 3 eggs, honey, yogurt; aquafaba would need testing.
- [ ] [[Filippos' Chocolate Brownies]]: 4 eggs and no oil; probably replace with a dedicated vegan brownie recipe (see Untranscribed Recipes).
- [ ] [[Challah]]: eggs give richness, colour & crumb; vegan challah (aquafaba, more oil, turmeric) is a different loaf.
- [ ] [[Crêpe Roses with Ricotta and Flambéed Orange Sauce]]: each swap is easy, but there are many of them together.

## Likely Not Veganisable

- [ ] [[Ice Cream]]: custard base; vegan ice cream needs a new base (coconut/cashew plus stabiliser). Keep the flavour ideas.
