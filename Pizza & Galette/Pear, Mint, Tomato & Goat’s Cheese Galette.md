# Ingredients

- 100g vegan goat's cheese (Fauxmage's is particularly good)
- All ingredients for galette from pg. 132 of Ottolenghi’s _Flavour_, except the sage
- 2 pears (Bosc variety are good)
- Fresh oreganum & mint
- A few teaspoons of vegan sour cream, or thick unsweetened plain plant yogurt
- 3 cloves of garlic
- Whole tomatoes, as small as possible
- 10 cardamom pods
- 1tsp (heaped) caraway seeds
- Cinnamon, cloves, and/or Chinese 5-spice
- Maple syrup or agave syrup (in place of honey)

# Method

1. Make galette from pg. 132 of Ottolenghi’s _Flavour_; substitute sage for oreganum.
2. Spread sliced pears on baking tray and spice with mix of cinnamon, cloves, and/or (mostly) Chinese 5 spice. Lightly drizzle with olive oil, and bake at 190 until soft.
3. Similarly roast tomatoes, drizzled in olive oil, salted and peppered.
4. Mix vegan goat's cheese with vegan sour cream, minced garlic, salt, pepper, olive oil, chopped mint, ground cardamom, and ground (toasted) caraway.
5. Assemble galette; brush with plant milk (mixed with a little oil or maple syrup, for colour) instead of egg wash, and bake as instructed in Ottolenghi, for a bit less time.
6. Drizzle with maple or agave syrup and add fresh mint leaves once cool.

# Notes

- Check the galette dough on pg. 132 of _Flavour_ for butter or other dairy; if present, substitute vegan block butter (and plant milk/yogurt as needed).
