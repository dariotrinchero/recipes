# Ingredients

## Cooked lentils

- 1 cup black lentils
- 1 cup mixed orange & brown lentils (~ 2/3 brown, 1/3 orange)
- herbs & spices, _ad lib_; consider
    - soy sauce, vegetable stock
    - cumin, coriander
    - onion & garlic powder
    - dried thyme & oregano
    - MSG, salt, pepper
- 1 white onion, roughly diced
- 3 large cloves of garlic, minced

## Patties

- cooked lentils, as above
- ½ cup (~45g) chickpea flour (binder, in place of egg whites; see Notes)
- 2 tbsp (~16g) vital wheat gluten (binder)
- up to ½ cup aquafaba (or water/stock), as needed for moisture
- ~1+1/4 cup fine bread crumbs, plus a little more by eye
- 3 white onions, sliced root-to-tip
- olive oil

## Burger sauce (all ingredients to taste)

- [[Vegan Mayonnaise]]
- sriracha
- mustard, wholegrain & Dijon
- soy sauce
- onion & garlic powder

## Burgers

- burger buns (check they're egg- and dairy-free)
- greens of choice (rocket recommended)
- vegan cheese of choice, such as [[Vegan Mozzarella]] or Fauxmage
- tomato
- red onions, pickled or raw

# Method

1. Cook lentils in minimal water. Mixture should be cooked through and slightly pasty once all excess water has cooked off. Microwave for ~6min to drive off some more water. Allow to cool.
2. Caramelise 3 onions while the lentils cook & cool. Drain of excess oil.
3. Sprinkle the chickpea flour and vital wheat gluten over the cooled lentils and mix thoroughly (a little vigorously, to help the gluten develop). Add aquafaba a splash at a time until the mixture is moist, then slowly add bread crumbs until it is tacky but holds together. Mix in caramelised onions. Rest in the fridge for at least 30 minutes, so the flours hydrate and the mixture firms up.
4. Form into patties & bake (on parchment + non-stick covering such as Spray & Cook) at 170 degrees for around 10-15min, until largely structurally sound (you should be able to gently handle complete patty with hands). Do not over-bake, or pressing patties down in pan becomes largely impossible.
5. Fry patties in oil over high heat in a stainless-steel skillet, until exterior is charred & crispy. Baste while frying, and flip once. Add layers of vegan cheese on top after first flip, then stack second fried patty on top of cheese.
6. Toast buns face-down in vegan butter over high heat.
7. Assemble burger, using sauce to hold ingredients together.

# Notes

- **Veganised (untested).** The binder is a best guess, scaled up from the one in [[Really Awesome Black Bean Burgers]] to replace the protein of 6-7 egg whites: chickpea flour sets when cooked much like egg, and vital wheat gluten adds chew and cohesion. Aquafaba replaces the moisture the egg whites provided (and binds a little itself). The initial bake also helps the chickpea flour set before frying.
- Binder alternatives to try (see also the notes in [[Really Awesome Black Bean Burgers]]):
    - **Methylcellulose**: roughly 1.5-2% of the mixture's weight, hydrated in cold water until it gels, in place of the chickpea flour and gluten.
    - **More vital wheat gluten** for a firmer, chewier patty; too much becomes rubbery.
    - **Chickpea flour only** (~¾ cup) for a gluten-free version, at the cost of some cohesion.
- Caramelised onions help a lot with both flavour and binding. See [this post on Reddit](https://www.reddit.com/r/vegetarian/comments/w2u048/i_finally_made_a_veggie_burger_that_doesnt_fall/).
- Despite my best efforts, these are probably not as good as J Kenji Lopez Alt's [[Really Awesome Black Bean Burgers]].
