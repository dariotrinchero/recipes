# Ingredients

## Tempura

- 200g purple sprouting broccoli, woody ends trimmed, chunky pieces halved
- 100g plain flour
- 50g cornflour
- 1Tbsp black sesame seeds (optional)
- 250ml soda water, ice-cold
- large pinch of salt
- vegetable oil, for deep-frying

## Nuoc cham

- 2Tbsp vegan fish sauce (see notes)
- juice of 2 limes
- 2Tbsp sugar
- 1 bird's-eye chilli, finely chopped
- 5Tbsp hot water

# Method

1. For the nuoc cham, whisk together all of the ingredients in a small bowl, and set aside while you make the tempura.
2. Whisk together the plain flour, cornflour, sesame seeds (if using), and a large pinch of salt. Fill a large, deep pan no more than a third full with oil, and heat to 180°C — a cube of bread dropped in should brown in 20s.
3. Quickly whisk the soda water into the flour mixture, being careful not to overmix.
4. Dunk the broccoli into the batter using tongs, lower carefully into the hot oil, and fry for 2-3min, until crisp.
5. Drain on kitchen paper, and serve with the nuoc cham alongside for dipping.

# Notes

- The original calls for ordinary fish sauce; vegan fish sauce works well here, as in [[Pad Thai]], or substitute soy sauce with an extra squeeze of lime.
- Tenderstem broccoli is a fine substitute when purple sprouting is out of season.
- The batter must be mixed at the last moment and left lumpy — overmixing, or letting it sit, costs the crispness.
