# Ingredients

- 200g shiitake mushrooms, stems trimmed, caps halved or thickly sliced
- 200g chestnut mushrooms, halved or thickly sliced
- 200g king oyster mushrooms, halved lengthwise & cut chunky
- ~170g vegetable oil (see notes)
- 150g plain flour
- 200g yellow onion, diced
- 150g green bell pepper (about 1.5 medium peppers), diced
- 100g celery, diced
- 4 cloves garlic, minced
- 1/2tsp dried thyme (or 1tsp fresh, to finish)
- 1/4-1/2tsp cayenne pepper, to taste
- 1/2tsp black pepper
- 1-1.5tsp salt, to taste
- 1.5L hot vegetable stock (or water)
- 2-3 scallion greens, finely sliced, to garnish

# Method

1. Heat a generous film of oil in a pot, and fry the mushrooms in batches until golden. Drain each batch, reserving the oil.
2. Weigh the reserved oil, topping it up to ~170g if needed, and return it to the pot. Stir in the flour, and cook over medium heat, stirring constantly, until the roux reaches a dark chocolate brown (15-20min).
3. Add the onion, bell pepper, and celery directly to the hot roux, and cook for 6-8min, until softened and lightly caramelised.
4. Stir in the garlic, thyme, cayenne, and black pepper, and cook for 1min until fragrant. Gradually add the stock, stirring to avoid clumps, and season lightly with salt.
5. Bring to a gentle simmer, and cook uncovered for 20-25min to let the flavours meld.
6. Return the browned mushrooms to the pot, and simmer a further 10-15min, so that they reabsorb liquid while staying meaty.
7. Adjust the seasoning, and stir in fresh thyme if using. Garnish with the scallion greens, and serve over hot rice with a dash of hot sauce.

# Notes

- The oil is about 10% heavier than the flour, rather than equal to it, to offset what the mushrooms absorb.
- Stop the roux earlier, at a milk chocolate colour, for a milder, less smoky gumbo.
- For extra umami, add 1-2tsp soy sauce along with the garlic.
